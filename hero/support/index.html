Hero Shot 使用支持

Hero Shot 用于在 iPhone 本机把截图或照片放进 3D 手机模型，合成展示图片。

如何开始

点左上角的图片按钮选择一张截图或照片，它会显示在手机屏幕里。然后通过左上角的工具菜单切换“英雄镜头 / 姿态 / 背景 / 画布”进行调整，完成后点右上角的导出按钮。

保存到相册

在导出面板中点“导出”，图片会保存到相册。

无法保存到相册

请在 iOS“设置 > 隐私与安全性 > 照片”中检查 Hero 的权限。授予“添加照片”权限后即可保存。

会员与恢复购买

Hero Pro 提供年度订阅和永久买断两种方式。更换设备或重新安装后，在会员页面点“恢复购买”即可恢复。年度订阅可在 iPhone 的“设置 > Apple 账户 > 订阅”中管理或取消，取消后可使用至当期结束。

退出后作品不见了

当前版本不保证恢复退出前的编辑进度，请及时导出重要作品。

如何删除数据

已导出的作品请在相册或“文件”中删除。卸载应用会清除本地偏好设置和应用沙盒数据，不会自动删除相册中的作品。

Hero Shot Support

Hero Shot turns your screenshots and photos into 3D iPhone mockups, entirely on your iPhone.

Getting started

Tap the photo button in the top-left corner to choose a screenshot or photo; it appears on the phone's screen. Use the tools menu in the top-left corner to switch between Hero Shots, Pose, Background and Canvas. When you're done, tap the export button in the top-right corner.

Saving to Photos

Tap “Export” in the export panel and the image is saved to your photo library.

Can't save to Photos

Check Hero's permission in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos. Once “Add Photos Only” access is granted, saving works.

Membership and restoring purchases

Hero Pro is available as an annual subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. After switching devices or reinstalling, tap “Restore Purchases” on the membership screen. Manage or cancel the annual subscription in iPhone Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions; after cancelling, Pro stays active until the end of the current period.

My work disappeared after closing the app

The current version does not guarantee that unsaved edits are restored after the app quits. Please export important work promptly.

Deleting your data

Delete exported images from Photos or Files. Uninstalling the app removes its local preferences and sandbox data, but does not delete images already saved to Photos.

Contact support

Email: 1602338218@qq.com. Please describe the issue and include the app version and your device model. Never send passwords or unnecessary personal information.
