Hero Shot 隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 9 月 25 日

Hero Shot（以下简称 Hero）是在 iPhone 本机把截图或照片放进 3D 手机模型、合成展示图片的工具。Hero 无需注册账号，不含广告，不接入第三方统计或追踪服务，也没有开发者运营的服务器接收你的数据。

图片处理

你通过系统照片选择器主动选择的图片只在设备上解码、编辑和渲染。Hero 不会扫描你的照片图库，也不会将原图或生成的图片上传到任何服务器。编辑中的素材只保留在应用运行内存中；退出应用后不保证恢复编辑进度，请及时导出重要作品。

保存到相册

保存到相册时，Hero 只申请“添加照片”权限，不读取相册中的其他内容。

购买与会员

Hero Pro 会员（年度订阅或永久买断）通过 Apple 的 App 内购买完成，付款由 Apple 处理。Hero 使用 Apple 的 StoreKit 在设备上验证会员状态，不会接收或保存你的 Apple 账户、银行卡或付款信息。订阅可随时在 iPhone 的“设置 > Apple 账户 > 订阅”中管理或取消。

本地偏好设置

Hero 在设备本地保存以下设置：震动反馈开关、屏幕壁纸选择、背景方式与强度、灵动岛显示选项、你保存的姿态、背景配色和画布尺寸。这些设置不包含你的照片，也不会离开设备。卸载应用会移除这些设置与临时文件，但不会删除已保存到相册或其他位置的作品。

权限管理

你可以随时在 iOS“设置”中管理 Hero 的照片添加权限。Hero 不需要位置、麦克风、相机或通讯录权限。

儿童隐私

Hero 不收集任何个人信息，包括儿童的个人信息。

政策变更

如本政策发生变化，我们会更新本页面及上方的更新日期。

Hero Shot Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Hero Shot (“Hero”) places your screenshots or photos into a 3D iPhone model and renders mockup images, entirely on your iPhone. Hero requires no account, contains no ads, uses no third-party analytics or tracking services, and has no developer-operated server that receives your data.

Image processing

Images you choose through the system photo picker are decoded, edited and rendered only on your device. Hero does not scan your photo library and never uploads your original or generated images to any server. Work in progress is kept only in the app's memory; edits are not guaranteed to be restored after the app quits, so please export important work promptly.

Saving to Photos

To save images, Hero requests “Add Photos Only” access and does not read anything else in your photo library.

Purchases and membership

Hero Pro (annual subscription or one-time lifetime purchase) is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase, and payment is handled by Apple. Hero uses Apple's StoreKit to verify your membership on the device and never receives or stores your Apple Account, card or payment information. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in iPhone Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions.

Local preferences

Hero stores the following settings locally on your device: haptic feedback, screen wallpaper, background style and strength, Dynamic Island visibility, your saved poses, background colors and canvas sizes. These settings contain none of your photos and never leave your device. Uninstalling the app removes these settings and temporary files, but does not delete images you have saved to Photos or elsewhere.

Permissions

You can manage Hero's photo-adding permission at any time in iOS Settings. Hero does not need location, microphone, camera or contacts access.

Children's privacy

Hero does not collect any personal information, including that of children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

For questions about this policy or data handling, contact 1602338218@qq.com. If you send us feedback by email, your email address, description and attachments are used only to handle that feedback. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.
